Finding Our Place

Poems about Self, Others and Nation. Four poems, but only one method — and once you have the method, an unseen poem stops being frightening.

The pattern behind your package

The poems are not a random set. They widen outwards, exactly as the booklet's subtitle promises — from one boy's identity, to how we grieve for someone else, to what a country does to its own past, to what it costs another country.

Self KentangIdentity forced on you, versus identity you choose.
Others Leave-TakingGrief performed loudly, versus grief that is real and silent.
Nation Singapore RiverProgress that succeeds, and erases a history doing it.
Nation + AlbatrossOur development, paid for with somebody else's land.

Every one of them runs on the same engine: a comparison held for the whole poem, which then turns. Learn to find the comparison and the turn, and you can open any of them — including one you have never seen.

📄 Keep your printed package beside you. Only short extracts are quoted here, for analysis — the poems themselves are in your booklet, and reading from paper is also what the exam will ask of you.
